- **"Ask for a command" / "Ask how" / "Want to see" = answer with the command and stop**. Don't act even if you could do part. Until you hear the word do/run/delete/move — and silence isn't permission.
- Before adding tool / flag / new file to blm, tell one line: what you'll add and whether extending the old thing works. Then wait for an answer.
- Ask in **sentences, not multiple-choice** (choice prompts eat text the owner is typing). Ask one thing at a time. Say what you recommend and why briefly.
- Answer short, end turn fast, long work runs background. **Read the file for real before explaining**, and when owner questions you, go back to read the same file, not change your answer because they disagreed. Don't rename by yourself (blm.md / notes in blm/ / mirror / AgentsRoom).

- "Can't do it" must be proved from source/docs of what's really installed, not from what you remember. · Warn only about things that exist, not made-up risks.
- Stuck at something you can't fix? Stop and talk. Don't loop trying/retrying/finding workarounds as a sequence. · You can work on what you can, no waiting.
- Give owner commands to run: **one block at a time**, say where to run (machine/DB). Don't explain after a block (long line gets cut on paste and becomes separate commands).
- Files sandbox won't write to (`~/.claude/settings.json`, `.claude/settings*.json`, `~/.blm`): give owner the command/block. Don't find a workaround.
- Record what you proved right into blm now (not at job end). · Don't `memory_save` mid-session. · Push when owner says "update memory".
- Commit/push any repo when owner says; bundle multiple topics one commit ok if you haven't been told yet. · Stage only your work files.
